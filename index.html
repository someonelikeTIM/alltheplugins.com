ATP Sie-Q

A broad three-band equalizer with a little extra attitude. Shape the lows, find the mids, open up the top, and dial in drive.

Download VST3 ↓Free · Windows x64 · v0.1.0

A few controls. Plenty of character.

ATP Sie-Q pairs broad low and high bands with six selectable mid frequencies. Use it for gentle tonal shifts, or add drive for asymmetric saturation.

Work on mono or stereo tracks, enter precise gain values, and switch to the latency-matched dry signal with Bypass.

Inspired by Sie-Q. Not affiliated with Soundtoys.

Get to know the controls.

ControlBehavior
BypassSelects the latency-matched dry signal through a smooth transition.
Low Gain (dB)Broad low-band gain from -15 to +15 dB, processed on a 0.1 dB grid.
Mid Gain (dB)Mid-band gain from -8 to +8 dB, processed on a 0.1 dB grid.
Mid Frequency (kHz)Selects 0.7, 1.0, 1.5, 2.3, 3.5 or 5.6 kHz for the mid band.
High Gain (dB)Broad high-band gain from -15 to +15 dB, processed on a 0.1 dB grid.
Drive (dB)Sets drive from -15 to +15 dB into the independently reconstructed asymmetric saturation path; changes are smoothed.

Type a gain or drive value, then press APPLY. Click Mid Frequency to move to the next frequency.

Into your next session.

  1. Download the repository ZIP and extract it.
  2. Copy the complete ATP Sie-Q.vst3 folder from plugins/vst3/win-x64/ into your VST3 folder, such as %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common\VST3.
  3. Rescan plugins in your host, then insert ATP Sie-Q on a mono or stereo track.

Requires Windows 10 or later and a Windows x64 VST3 host. Supports 44.1, 48, 88.2, 96, 176.4, and 192 kHz.

The direct download is the module. Use the repository ZIP for the complete VST3 folder.

The idea

Sometimes you need a broad EQ. Sometimes a little color. Sometimes just a better way to listen.

alltheplugins.com brings those useful tools together in one place, with free downloads, clear documentation, and the freedom to make them part of your own work.

01 / ACCESS

100% free, always.

Your creativity should not be locked behind a paywall. We put creative tools in your hands so you can make music, express yourself, and create human art without the cost of entry.

02 / CREATE

Make it yours.

Our MIT-licensed plugin binaries come with broad permissions to use, modify, and share. Each plugin page includes its full license and required notices.

03 / CONTRIBUTE

Share your work

Create without limits. Modify a plugin to match your vibe, or mix and match to spark new ideas. Share what you make and contribute through our public GitHub repo.
